Microtask Queue : 
================
→ The Microtask Queue is a high-priority queue.
Tasks from **Promises** and **Mutation Observers** go into the microtask queue.

→ Microtask Queue is a high-priority waiting area for promise callbacks, and JavaScript runs these tasks immediately after the 
current code finishes in the call stack—before any other callbacks.
